# 50-Question Privacy Questionnaire

The questionnaire is implemented in `backend/services/questionnaire.py`.

Categories:
A. Profile Visibility (Q1-Q4)
B. Personal Information (Q5-Q11)
C. Location Privacy (Q12-Q17)
D. Posts & Content (Q18-Q22)
E. Friends / Followers (Q23-Q25)
F. Tagging & Mentions (Q26-Q29)
G. Authentication & Account Security (Q30-Q36)
H. Third-Party Apps (Q37-Q40)
I. Messaging & Social Engineering (Q41-Q45)
J. Digital Footprint (Q46-Q50)

The application asks whether information is visible or whether a behavior occurs; it does not request the actual sensitive information.
