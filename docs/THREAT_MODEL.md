# Defensive Threat Model

| Asset | Threat | Exposure | Potential Impact | Existing Control | Recommended Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Account | Account takeover | Weak authentication practices | Unauthorized access | MFA question | Enable MFA, login alerts |
| Identity information | Impersonation | Public profile context | Fraud/confusion | Visibility controls | Limit unnecessary public information |
| Contact information | Phishing | Public phone/email | Unwanted contact | Privacy settings | Reduce visibility |
| Location privacy | Location exposure | Real-time posts/check-ins | Safety/privacy impact | Location questions | Avoid real-time public sharing |
| Private content | Unwanted disclosure | Tagging/public posts | Privacy loss | Tag review question | Enable review controls |
| Social relationships | Social engineering | Public connections/context | More convincing scams | Awareness questions | Verify unfamiliar contacts |
| Third-party access | App exposure | Unused integrations | Unnecessary data access | App-review questions | Revoke unused permissions |

Risk depends on context; this matrix is educational rather than a professional risk determination.
